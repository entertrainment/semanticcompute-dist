01 / WITHOUT SEMANTICCOMPUTE

Inspect the output

The candidate is plausible at normal scale. A few isolated bad channels and rounding differences are easy to miss, especially in a large texture or a long render sequence.

02 / WITH SEMANTICCOMPUTE

Measure and localise

Apply a stated tolerance, count every mismatch, locate the first and worst pixel/channel, and distinguish small ULP drift from a NaN or a genuine algorithmic divergence.

03 / AFTER THE FIX

Re-verify the result

The package does not edit a shader. It makes the failure inspectable, supplies a likely remedy, and checks the developer's corrected output against the same trusted reference.

What the numbers differentiate

Switch between exact comparison and ULP(1). The tolerance policy matters: a one-step Float32 rounding difference may be acceptable in a stated contract; a NaN or a large numerical change is not hidden by that choice.
